Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We handle the setup, and once it is live we show you how to use it. If something needs changing, just message us on WhatsApp.

Is the Digital Shop a one-time payment?

Yes — the Digital Shop price is a one-time setup fee. Extra product photos beyond the first 10 are ₹500 for every additional 10.

Can I update products and prices later?

Yes. Products, prices and categories can be updated whenever your stock or rates change.

Does the UPI QR checkout confirm payments automatically?

The QR fills in the exact order amount so the customer just scans and pays — but payment confirmation still happens on your UPI app, the same way you check any UPI payment today. There are no gateway charges.

How are Excel automation, marketing and custom tools priced?

These depend on what you need, so we give a fixed quote after a short chat about your requirement. You know the price before any work starts.

Can I take more than one service together?

Yes — many businesses start with a Digital Shop and add reels or automation later. Tell us what you need and we will put together a combined quote.
